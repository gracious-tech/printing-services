# Services research
Notes about various printing services.

**This module does not support all these services. This is simply a record of research done.**


## Print-anything services

An evaluation of popular printing services in terms of their suitability for printing single copies of third-party or generated books for personal use rather than publishing.

| Service       | Print anything easily | Create for free   | One copy cost ok  | Cost      | API   |
|---------------|-----------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|-------|
| KDP           | No                    | Yes               | Yes               | Low       | No    |
| Lulu          | Yes                   | Yes               | Yes               | Medium    | Yes   |
| BookVault     | Maybe                 | No                | Yes               | Low       | Yes   |
| Blurb         | Yes                   | Yes               | Yes               | High      | Yes   |
| IngramSpark   | No                    | Yes               | Yes               | Medium    | No    |
| Draft2Digital | No                    | No                | Yes               | Medium    | No    |
| Mixam         | Confusing             | Yes               | Yes (not for AU)  | Medium    | Yes   |

Ignored: The Book Patch (doesn't accept PDF cover)
